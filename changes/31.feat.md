Assemble the triage LangGraph StateGraph (M2-5b) from M2-5a's node functions: 11 nodes (ingest, classify_intent, escalation_check, resolve, draft, regulatory_guard, human_approval, guard_final, send, human_review, escalate), compiled with interrupt_before=['human_approval'] and a SqliteSaver checkpointer. Resuming after the pause requires injecting a HumanDecision via update_state() -- a bare resume with no decision raises ValueError rather than silently defaulting to approve. The draft is checked against the guard twice (regulatory_guard before the pause, guard_final after resume) so an edited draft is re-validated before send. Fixed real bugs found while assembling the graph: escalation_check_node called .get() on Turn pydantic objects (no such method); HumanDecision was missing from the checkpoint serde allowlist, silently deserializing as a plain dict after any real resume and breaking human_approval routing; resolve_node's ThreadPoolExecutor timeout was defeated by the context manager's __exit__ unconditionally calling shutdown(wait=True). Checkpoint files are created 0600 before sqlite3 opens them, since they carry redacted patient data and human decisions.
