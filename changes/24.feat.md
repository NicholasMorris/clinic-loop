Cohort generator and templated message corpus for bias evaluation.

Adds equal-base-rate synthetic cohorts distinguished by planted confounds (household-shared instruments, renter address churn, transliteration variance, first-of-January DOBs, multigenerational instruments). Ground truth is exported separately from case records, which omit demographic attributes. Configuration is read from `src/clinicloop/world/config/cohorts.toml` with validation. Templated message corpus builder generates messages labelled with intent and escalation flags for the triage golden set.
