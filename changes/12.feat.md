Text-to-speech spike harness for measuring performance and selecting audio generation strategy. Implements `SpikeReport` pydantic model with measured fields, `select_branch()` decision logic for three branches (per-turn synthesis, whole-dialogue + alignment, limited corpus), `run_isolated_worker()` for offline isolated TTS invocation, `write_sample()` with SHA-256 verification, and `ManifestEntry` validation enforcing synthetic-only audio. Includes comprehensive test suite and documentation.
